# Chromaze

## Platform and stack
Native iOS and Android, built with Expo SDK 57, React Native and TypeScript. The user's Expo Go app supports SDK 57; preserve that compatibility. Skia renders the game; Gesture Handler interprets swipes; Reanimated drives motion; AsyncStorage persists unlocks.

## Purpose
An original puzzle game where each swipe sends a ball to the next wall, painting every floor tile it crosses. Success is a responsive, playable three-level prototype with synchronized motion and paint, reliable undo/restart, and persistent progression.

## Constraints
Use AMAZE!!! as a mechanical reference only. Original layouts and graphics. One swipe means one uninterrupted slide; input during a slide is ignored. No monetization, accounts, multiplayer, procedural levels, or advanced puzzle mechanisms in this milestone.

## Brand commitments
Chromaze; modern dark presentation, background #111827, walls #2F3B52, mint paint #5CD6C0, lavender ball #A78BFA, amber accent #F5B971. Maze and responsiveness take priority over decoration.

## Open decisions
Long-term audience, sound/haptics, difficulty targets, and accessibility alternatives to swiping have not been specified.
